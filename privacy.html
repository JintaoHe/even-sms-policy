Privacy Policy

JT (Sole Proprietor) · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers SMS notifications sent from +1 (331) 320-5495 by Even, a personal assistant app operated under the registered Brand JT. Even is currently in development and testing, and will be offered to users who sign up for it.

Who receives messages

Messages are sent only to Even users (including test users) who have opted in by texting START to +1 (331) 320-5495.

Information collected

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Your opt-in and opt-out status, including the date of each.
  • The content of notifications sent to you and any replies you send (for example STOP or HELP).

How information is used

Your information is used only to send the notifications you set up in Even (calendar and birthday reminders, a short daily summary of your schedule, and travel updates), to respond to your replies, and to honor opt-out requests. It is never used for marketing or advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with the messaging provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages, or when required by law.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one final confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention and security

Information is kept only as long as needed to provide the service and is stored with reasonable safeguards. After an opt-out, your number is kept only on an opt-out list so you are not messaged again.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reply HELP to any message from +1 (331) 320-5495.
