Privacy Policy

JT (Sole Proprietor) · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers SMS notifications sent from +1 (331) 320-5495 by Even, a personal assistant app built and run as a hobbyist developer project under the registered Brand JT. This is not a commercial service.

Who receives messages

Messages are sent only to the developer's own mobile phone, after opting in by texting START to +1 (331) 320-5495.

Information collected

  • The recipient's mobile phone number.
  • Opt-in and opt-out status, including the date of each.
  • The content of messages sent by the app and any replies (for example STOP or HELP).

How information is used

This information is used only to send the notifications the app generates (calendar and birthday reminders, a short daily summary and travel updates), to respond to replies, and to honor opt-out requests. It is never used for marketing or advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with the messaging provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages, or when required by law.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. One final confirmation message is sent, and then no further messages. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention and security

Information is kept only as long as needed to run the app and is stored with reasonable safeguards. After an opt-out, the number is kept only on an opt-out list so it is not messaged again.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reply HELP to any message from +1 (331) 320-5495.
