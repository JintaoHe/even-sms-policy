This Privacy Policy explains how Luke He ("I", "me"), operating the personal assistant app Even under the registered Brand Even A2P / Luke He, collects and uses information in connection with text messages (SMS/MMS) sent from the phone number +1 (331) 320-5495.

Who receives messages

This is a personal, non-commercial service. Messages are sent only to me and to a small number of family members and friends who know me personally and have verbally agreed to receive texts.

Information collected

  • Your name and mobile phone number, provided when you agree to receive messages.
  • Your consent (opt-in) status, including the date you opted in or opted out.
  • The content of messages sent to you and any replies you send (for example STOP or HELP).

How information is used

Your information is used only to send you the reminders and updates you agreed to receive (such as event or birthday reminders, a short daily summary and travel updates), to respond to your replies, and to honor opt-out requests. It is never used for marketing or advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with the messaging provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages, or when required by law.

Opting out

You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one final confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention and security

Information is kept only as long as needed to provide the service and is stored with reasonable safeguards. If you opt out, your number is kept only on an opt-out list so you are not messaged again.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reply HELP to any message from +1 (331) 320-5495.
